Department of Computer Science

Department Information

A computer science department is an academic unit that teaches computing foundation, conducts research in advanced technologies and prepare students for careers across software, data, and emerging tech fields.

Courses offered

BCA

The Bachelors of Computer Application is a 3 year Under Graduate Program Degree, focused on Computer Science, Software Development and Information Technology.

Subjects covered in the course are-
Programming Languages: C++, Java, Python
Database Management System(DBMS): SQL and PS
Software Engineering: Operating Systems and System Design

M.Sc. Computer Science

M.Sc. Computer Science is a 2 year Post Graduate Program Degree, focused on advanced topics in Computing, software development and Theoritical Research.

Subject covered in courses are--
Advanced Data Structure
Theoretical Computer Science
Computer Networks
